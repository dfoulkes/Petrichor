Irrigation Controller — Wiring & Cable Schedule

Single-tap IoT irrigation controller: an ESP32 (ESPHome) switching an 8-channel relay that gates 24 V AC to solenoid valves. Two DIN supplies off one mains feed. Every terminal and cable grade below is taken from the manufacturer datasheets in datasheets/ — this is a build guide, not a certified electrical drawing.

Feed: 230 V AC · UK plug, 5 A fuse as supplied · RCD socket Rails: 5 V DC logic · 24 V AC valves (SELV) Total draw: ~0.32 A @ 230 V Rev: 2026-07-24

⚠ Corrections in this revision — the previous drawing was wrong

  • The HDR-15-5 has no earth terminal. Rev 2026-07-19 routed earth to HDR ⏚ (FG). That terminal does not exist. Datasheet p.1: "Isolation class Ⅱ"; the pin table lists exactly four pins — +V, −V, AC/N, AC/L. Earth now terminates in its own Wago and bonds to the DIN rail.
  • The plug fuse is 5 A, not 3 A. The supplied lead (CPC PL17788 / Pro Elec PELB2340) ships with 5 A, which correctly protects 0.75 mm² flex (6 A rated). Leave it alone.
  • Wago count is 7, not 2 — L, N, E on the mains side; +5V and 0V on the logic side; two for the valve tails.

02Topology

One 230 V feed lands on a three-way Wago bank, which splits Live and Neutral to both supplies and parks Earth on the rail. 5 V runs the logic; the relay switches 24 V AC out to the valve and back. The dashed boundary is the mains/SELV segregation line.

🚫 Why 22 AWG must never touch the mains side

The plug fuse is 5 A. That fuse protects everything downstream of it. 22 AWG carries roughly 3 A — so under a fault the wire becomes the fuse element, melting its insulation and igniting before a 5 A fuse reacts. An RCD will not save you: it is not an earth fault, just a wire cooking in a sealed box.

The HDR-15 manual §3 lists 18 AWG / 0.8 mm² as the smallest permitted size. 22 AWG is not on the table.

🌡 The 70 °C question — bench vs garden

HDR manual §4: "Use wires that can withstand temperatures of at least 80 °C, such as UL1007." 3183Y flex cores are 70 °C — a real deviation, but at 0.25 A through 0.75 mm² the self-heating is negligible, so it is a non-issue on the bench.

It matters outdoors: a sealed IP65 box in summer sun reaches 50–60 °C internally, plus the PSU's own ~3 W. That eats the margin on 70 °C insulation. 105 °C tri-rated does not care. Bench on flex; re-wire in tri-rated before it goes outside.

06Things that catch people out

🔍 Verify the lead cores — don't trust colour

It's a moulded plug, so you can't see inside. Pull the fuse. Blue should beep to the left-hand pin, green/yellow to the long top pin, and brown should beep to nothing — the fuse sits in the Live path. Refit the fuse and brown beeps to the right-hand pin. That's proof, not assumption.

✂️ Splitting a sheathed flex for its cores

Electrically fine — the cores are 300/500 V insulated. Two rules: the sheath must pass through the gland and stop just inside the box (the sheath is the mechanical protection at the entry), and score it lengthways to open it — never ring-cut and drag, you'll nick a core.

⚡ Getting 24 V from the transformer

Take 5↔7. Terminal 6 is a centre tap — measure across 5–6 or 6–7 and you'll read 12 V and think it's broken. Measure 5↔7 and you get 24 V AC.

🔌 Mounting orientation is not optional

HDR manual §2: "the input terminals at the bottom … Mounting orientations other than that … is not allowed." Vertical, mains at the bottom, 5 V at the top. Convection cooling and free segregation. Clearances: 40 mm above, 20 mm below, 5 mm sides.

⚠ Mains safety — read before wiring the 230 V side

  • Plug out of the wall for all of it. HDR manual warning (9): disconnect before any installation or modification.
  • 5 A fuse as supplied — correct for 0.75 mm² (6 A rated). Total draw ~0.32 A. RCD-protected socket upstream.
  • Neither supply has an earth terminal — both Class II. Earth terminates in its own Wago and bonds to the DIN rail. Never cut the earth core off and never leave it floating.
  • Do not wire the HDR's centre front screw — that's the Vo ADJ trimmer, turn-only. Set it to 5.00 V once powered.
  • Do not bridge primary and secondary. HDR manual warning (2): "Connecting both the primary and the secondary sides together is not allowed."
  • Deburr the cut DIN rail before bonding earth to it — that edge is sharp and terminals slide on it.
  • Measure before trusting: PSU ~5 V DC, transformer ~24 V AC, and L–N open-circuit before first power-up. A short means stop.
  • If you're not confident on the mains side, have a competent person check it. This is a guide, not a certified electrical drawing.
